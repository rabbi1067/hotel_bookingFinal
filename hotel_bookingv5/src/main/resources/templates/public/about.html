A destination in itself

Since 2016, Grand Meridian Resort has welcomed travellers from more than 60 countries to a quiet stretch of shoreline. Our philosophy is simple: anticipate, don't intrude.

Our story

Ten years beside the bay

2016

Opening season

Grand Meridian opened with 96 rooms, a staff of 140 and a simple promise of unhurried luxury on Cox's Bazar's longest beach.

2019

The skyline wing

Our rooftop lounge and 42-room skyline wing opened, adding a new perspective of the coastline.

2022

Wellness first

The rooftop spa, fitness studio and ayurvedic menu launched, placing wellbeing at the heart of every stay.

2025

Going digital

We rebuilt the entire guest journey online — bookings, payments, room service and check-in — one seamless experience.
